## Area Takeaway

In 2023+, the strongest thread in "PCA/LDA inside deep learning for image recognition" has moved away from training big whitening-integrated backbones and toward attaching classical supervised/unsupervised projections to **frozen pretrained backbones** (ResNet/ViT/DINOv2), where LDA provably beats full-feature linear probes on coarse-grained benchmarks ([40][41]); the other active line is **differentiable whitening layers inside the network** (ZCA-whitening before the classifier for imbalanced classification, arXiv 2024) ([42]). The best fit for this assignment is clearly (b)-type: *Supervised Dimensionality Reduction Revisited: Why LDA on Frozen CNN Features Deserves a Second Look* (arXiv:2604.03928, 2026) — official MIT-licensed PyTorch/scikit-learn code, 6 frozen backbones, CIFAR-100/TinyImageNet/CUB-200, feature extraction "~5 min per backbone on MPS/CUDA", reproducible end-to-end on an RTX 4060 in a few hours ([40][41]). Runner-ups: Whitening-Net (differentiable ZCA layer, arXiv:2408.17197 — novel but ImageNet-LT-scale training is heavy; CIFAR-LT-100 variant is feasible) ([42]) and Omae & Takahashi's CNN-with-PCA-layer + PCA-Grad-CAM (arXiv:2508.11880) ([43]).

## Sources

[40] I. Kumar, G. Karhana, S.K. Jasti, A.H. Lade — "Supervised Dimensionality Reduction Revisited: Why LDA on Frozen CNN Features Deserves a Second Look" — https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03928 — accessed 2026-11-16 — Tier: 1 (arXiv preprint, cs.LG/cs.CV)
Summary: LDA before linear probing on six frozen backbones (ResNet-18/50, MobileNetV3-Small, EfficientNet-B0, ViT-B/16, DINOv2-ViT-S/14) across CIFAR-100, Tiny ImageNet, CUB-200-2011. Beats full features in 11/12 coarse-grained configs (+up to 4.5 pp, −48–87% dimensionality), consistently loses on fine-grained CUB. Compared against PCA, PCA+LDA, regularized LDA (Ledoit–Wolf), LFDA, NCA, and three of the authors' own extensions.
Key quotes: "LDA improves accuracy over full features in 11 of 12 coarse-grained configurations, with gains up to 4.5 percentage points while reducing feature dimensionality by 48-87%"; "Code available at https://github.com/IndarKarhana/lda-image-classification".
