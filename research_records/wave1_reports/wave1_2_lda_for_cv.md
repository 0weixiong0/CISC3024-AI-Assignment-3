## Area Takeaway

The 2023+ LDA-for-CV landscape splits into three strands: (a) classical-variant papers published in linear-algebra/venue journals (orthogonal/uncorrelated, sparse, graph-embedded LDA) that are incremental and often lack code or recent image benchmarks; (b) shrinkage/regularized LDA for small-sample high-dimensional data, where the strongest recent work either predates the 2023 cutoff or is statistics-first rather than CV-first; and (c) the most promising strand — **LDA repurposed as a closed-form adapter/head on top of frozen deep or foundation-model embeddings**. Two candidates stand out for local reproducibility: **GO-LDA** (arXiv 2305.14568, May 2023), which corrects a claimed decades-old flaw in multiclass LDA and is verified here on image data (ORL, LFW, ResNet18 retinal/brain features) with pure NumPy-scale compute, and **Koo-Fu CLIP** (arXiv 2602.01127, Feb 2026), a Fukunaga-Koontz LDA adaptation of CLIP that lifts ImageNet-1K top-1 from 75.1% to 79.1% with zero gradient training — the strongest "beats deep at a fraction of the cost" claim found. GO-LDA is the safest pick (small data, CPU-feasible, no official code so reimplementation is clean); Koo-Fu CLIP is the highest-impact but requires ImageNet inference (feasible but multi-hour on an RTX 4060 if restricted to subsets).
