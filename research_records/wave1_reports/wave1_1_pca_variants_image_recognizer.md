## Area Takeaway

The 2023+ "new PCA variant" literature that is genuinely evaluated as an image recognizer splits into two very different streams: (a) a mature, fast-moving but code-shy stream of robust/tensor 2DPCA variants published in IEEE/Elsevier journals (e.g., DLR-2DPCA, IEEE TCE 2025), where the math is novel and the benchmarks are classic face/SSS image sets but official repositories are effectively absent; and (b) a smaller, more reproducible stream of open-access papers that release code (RSSPCA, PLOS ONE 2025, MATLAB + GPL-3.0 repo with the benchmark data matrices pre-packaged). A third pattern — PCA/kPCA bolted onto a CNN (kernel CNN-PCFF, Sci. Reports 2024; PCA + 2D-CNN hyperspectral, Sensors 2025) — is easy to run locally but the "new PCA algorithm" contribution is thin, so it is better treated as a hybrid fallback than as a PCA-family candidate. For a laptop-only reimplementation with verified numbers, **RSSPCA (2025)** is the standout, with **DLR-2DPCA (2025)** as the strongest "genuinely new PCA" option if the student accepts implementing from the paper alone, and the ACM 2024 **Global-Local 2D2PCA** as a simpler tensor-PCA alternative.
